  • Policy library is outdated; several standards are more than 18 months old.
  • Access reviews are overdue, with exceptions tracked in unmanaged spreadsheets.
  • Database key rotation is inconsistent; ownership unclear.
  • Third-party onboarding is fast-tracked for ‘low spend’ vendors without risk assessments.
  • SIEM monitoring produces noisy alerts that are often ignored.
